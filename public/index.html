SplitDeck plays up to 8 streams or videos side by side: YouTube, TikTok LIVE, Twitch, Kick, X, Facebook and most sites yt-dlp supports. Paste a link and press Enter. Hover a player for its controls; 📡 Live lists live channels, 🔎 Find looks for stream links in a page.

One player: its number, then a key

Each player shows its number at the top left. Press the number (1–8), then within 2 seconds:

FFull screen this player, with only its sound on
MMute / unmute it
SSolo: hear only this one
P or SpacePause / play
RReload it
EnterFocus: show only this player
CClose it

Example: 2 then F puts player 2 in full screen. Keys don't work while you type in a box, or right after clicking inside a YouTube or other embedded player (click an empty spot first).
